## Assets (exact output paths)
Regions `whisperpine` (deep greens, fireflies, moss, dusk) and `glass_dunes` (amber sand, crystal spires, heat haze, deeper dusk):
- `assets/regions/<id>/far.jpg`: 16:9 wide backdrop, distant landscape, NO characters, horizon about 55% down, no sky detail needed in the top 25% (code draws the sky; a soft gradient is fine).
- `assets/regions/<id>/mid.png`: 16:9 midground strip (tree line / dunes with crystal spires) occupying the lower ~60%, top area must be transparent.
- `assets/regions/<id>/fg.png`: 16:9 foreground framing elements (ferns, rocks, crystals) along the bottom edge and corners only, the rest transparent.
  For mid/fg: generate on a perfectly flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) background and chroma-key it out yourself with sharp (node script in your scratchpad dir; `sharp` is in node_modules once DEV finishes T-01, else `npm i sharp` in the scratchpad dir). If keying leaves bad fringes, use `remove_background` (check its cost first; log it).
- Heroes: ONE 2×2 sheet, side view facing RIGHT, full body, idle battle stance, same scale, generous spacing, on flat pure magenta #FF00FF. Order: top-left Bram, top-right Lysa, bottom-left Oren, bottom-right Kit. Crop each into `assets/raw/heroes/<id>_sprite.png` (key magenta → transparent, trim to bounding box). ids: bram, lysa, oren, kit.
- Portraits: ONE 2×2 sheet of chest-up portraits (3/4 view, expressive faces, painterly HD-2D character art, dark warm vignette background, same order). Crop into `assets/heroes/<id>_portrait.jpg` (square, ~512 px).
Keep each file ≤ 1.5 MB (jpg for opaque images, png for transparent ones; you may downscale to 1920 wide).

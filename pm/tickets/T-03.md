# T-03 — ART: 3 key-art candidates

**Role:** ART · **Owned paths:** assets/**, docs/art/**
**Inputs:** CLAUDE.md, pm/STATE.md, pm/CREDITS.md

## Goal
Generate 3 key-art candidates for the title screen with Higgsfield (tools are `mcp__Higgsfield__*`; load schemas via ToolSearch, e.g. "select:mcp__Higgsfield__estimate_image_cost,mcp__Higgsfield__generate_image_batch,mcp__Higgsfield__jobs_wait").

## Steps
1. `estimate_image_cost` for model `nano_banana_pro`, aspect_ratio 16:9, resolution 2k. Expected 2 credits each → 6 total. If the total would exceed 8, STOP and report.
2. One `generate_image_batch` call with 3 different prompts (each starts with the ART STYLE BLOCK from CLAUDE.md). Subject: a caravan of four heroes (armored warden with tower shield, hooded ranger with bow, robed ember priest with censer, nimble duelist with twin blades) and an old woman wagon driver beside a small wooden lantern-wagon holding a glowing living flame, on a winding road through a darkening fantasy world toward a distant ruined golden city with a giant dim brazier; warm ember light vs cold blue dusk; leave calm space in the upper-middle for a title logo. Vary composition: (a) side-on diorama of the road, (b) high three-quarter view over the woods-to-dunes landscape, (c) close low angle on the wagon lantern with heroes silhouetted.
   Do NOT pass folder_id. If the tool asks about unlim, choose credits (use_unlim false).
3. `jobs_wait` until done. Download each result with curl to `assets/key_art/candidate_a.png`, `_b`, `_c` (convert to PNG if needed; keep ≤ 2 MB each — you may use `npx -y sharp-cli` or a node one-liner with sharp from a temp dir if needed; or just keep jpg/webp with correct extension).
4. Log prompts + job ids in `docs/art/PROMPTS.md` and the spend in `pm/CREDITS.md` (add table rows; update the "Project spend so far" total).
5. Record each candidate's job_id in docs/art/PROMPTS.md — the PM will use the chosen job id as the style bible image_reference.

## Acceptance
3 images on disk, logged. Report the file paths, job ids and actual credits spent.

## Out of scope
Any other asset.

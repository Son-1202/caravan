## What was built
- **Dialogue** `src/ui/dialogue.ts`: dimmed overlay over the live diorama. Mira and mira_child portraits sit on the left; the party sits on the right. Each line has a name plate and typewriter text. Click, tap, Space or Enter first completes the line, then advances to the next. Esc and the Skip button end the scene. The Ember speaker gets a glowing flame and italic text; the narrator gets no portrait. CG timing: `cg/twist` appears from line 1 of after_r3_twist ("I was the Ember"), and `cg/epilogue` appears from line 4 of the epilogue. Bots skip all story scenes.
- **Title** `src/ui/screens/title.ts`:
  - Ken Burns pan on the key art, CSS ember particles and a glowing title.
  - A "Tap to start" prompt unlocks audio.
  - Buttons: Begin Journey, Hard Mode (locked until the first win, with a tooltip), Best Runs, and Settings (quality Auto/High/Low, volume, default speed 1x/2x).
  - The old `start.ts` was removed.
- **End screens** `src/ui/screens/end.ts`: time, fights won, breaks, ignites, damage, region reached, seed (marked Hard on hard runs), relic icons, best-run rank badge, and New Run / Title buttons.
- **Save** `src/game/save.ts`: stored in localStorage under `coe.save.v1`, with every access wrapped in try/catch. It holds the top 5 runs (wins first, then fastest), total wins and runs, `hardUnlocked` and settings. Hard mode creates the run with `new Run(seed, true)`.
- **Audio** `src/audio/{engine,sfx,music,index}.ts`: pure WebAudio with no new dependencies. It has every sound the ticket lists, plus map ambience with per-region accents and battle and boss drones. Volume follows the settings. It stays muted until the first user gesture.
- **Relic icons** were already used in the top bar, reward screen and merchant. They now also appear on the end screen and in Best Runs.
- **Mobile**: phone-landscape rules in `src/ui/story.css` and a portrait "rotate your device" overlay (`src/ui/rotate.ts`).
